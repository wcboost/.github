# WCBoost

**Build better WooCommerce stores.**

WCBoost makes WooCommerce plugins that improve how customers browse, compare,
and buy, and that keep things simple for the store owners who run them.

We started out of 10+ years of building and supporting WooCommerce plugins and themes.
Over that time we saw the same problems again and again: clunky product
selection, poor product discovery, and plugins that slow a store down. We
build one focused plugin for each of those problems.

## What we care about

- **Customer experience first.** Every feature should make shopping easier.
- **Lightweight by default.** Clean code, no bloat, no slowdown.
- **Works with your store.** Follows WooCommerce standards and fits well-coded themes.
- **Built for the long run.** Regular updates and real support.
